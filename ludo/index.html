How to play Alquida Landu

Goal: get all 4 of your tokens into the middle first.

  • 🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a token out. A 6 also means roll again.
  • 🐷 Three 6s in a row? Too greedy. Turn over.
  • ⚔️ Land on an enemy to send it back to its yard, and roll again.
  • ⭐ Star squares and start squares are safe from normal attacks.
  • 🏠 You need the exact number to get home. Getting home = roll again.

💣 KABOOM (self-destruct)

Everyone gets one Kaboom per game. Before you roll, if an enemy is within 2 squares of one of your tokens, you can blow that token up. It goes back to your yard, and so does every enemy within 2 squares. Stars don't protect anyone from a bomb.

❓ Mystery boxes

Stop on a box and something crazy happens:

  • 🚀 Rocket: +5 squares
  • 🍌 Banana: slip back 3
  • 🌀 Teleport: swap with a random enemy
  • 🛡️ Shield: can't be killed for a round
  • 🎲 Free roll
  • 💤 Sleeping pill: skip your next turn
  • 💣 Extra Kaboom charge

🤡 The Landu badge

Whoever has been killed the most wears the clown badge. Shame!
