Lyra for a Korean home

Korean pharmacies hand out a strip of dose pouches, not bottles. Lyra weighs the pouch as the unit, knows which time's pouch left the tray, and speaks polite Korean.

김순자 · 78 · lives alone in an apartment in Daegu

Her daughter 지은 lives in Seoul and hears from Lyra on KakaoTalk. Korea became a super-aged society in December 2024, with more than 20% of residents aged 65 or over (Ministry of the Interior and Safety resident registration data).

03 · Screens in Korean

Polite, short, and big enough to read from the kitchen

The text uses 존댓말, the polite form, as a grandchild would speak. Units follow Korean usage: 포 for pouches and sticks, 알 for tablets. Korean phrases are pre-rendered bitmaps from Noto Sans KR, so the device doesn't need a full Hangul font.

04 · What changes for Korea

Rules the firmware follows for these packages

The pouch is the unit

A pouch holds every pill for one time, so Lyra counts pouches, not pills. Each time's pouch weight is learned from its first three takes, using the median.

Wrong time's pouch

One pouch varies by about 50 mg, so a pouch matches its time within ±0.15 g. Lyra says “저녁 약봉투예요” only when the weight matches another time's pouch and is at least 0.3 g from the one due now. Weight is evidence; the printed label decides, so a press still accepts it if the label is right.

Never silently wrong

If the weight matches no pouch clearly, Lyra asks her to read the time printed on the pouch. Until she presses “네”, it counts as a pouch to put back. Two pouches stuck together are caught by their summed weight.

A tray, not a cup

The strip lies flat on a clip-in tray across two load cells, which Lyra reads as one bay. It is never lifted, so the tray counts as empty only below 0.3 g. The last 1.1 g pouch still counts.

Pouch strips aren't topped up

A large weight increase on the tray means a new strip. Lyra asks “새 약봉투인가요?” and takes the count from the label, usually 42 for 14 days × 3.

Times follow the pharmacy label

식후 30분 becomes a due time about 30 minutes after her usual mealtime, set once on the setup page. After a week, personal timing moves each reminder toward when she actually takes it.

Supplements stay quiet

Red ginseng, omega-3 and calcium: the cup glows and Lyra chimes once. Skips are logged but never sent to her daughter. The weekly summary lists them separately.

What has to be true in hardware: the tray reads a pouch to about ±50 mg, and pouches for different times differ by at least 0.3 g. When a prescription gives two times identical pouches, Lyra can't tell them apart by weight and falls back to the printed-label check. The setup page tells the pharmacist when that happens.

06 · Korea specifics

Messages, language and approvals

NeedLyra Korea
Family messagesKakaoTalk, sent through a registered business channel (알림톡). SMS is the fallback. The family installs nothing new.
VoicePrompts recorded in 존댓말 by a native speaker. Holding the knob reads the screen aloud, which helps with low vision.
SetupA Korean setup web page opened from the QR code. The pharmacist or family enters the pouch times, 14-day count and supplements.
Care workersA second recipient can be added, for example a local elderly-care worker, with the same alerts as the family.
Power and approvalsUSB-C with a Korean 220 V adapter. Needs KC certification for the radio and a review by 식약처 (MFDS) on how it is classified.

Code: the pouch, stick and supplement logic is in lyra/firmware/lib/lyra_core; the double-width tray is a bay map in src/main.cpp. The full spec is on the main Lyra page.
