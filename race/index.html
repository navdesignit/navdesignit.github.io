How to play Pon Pon Prix

Race the gang through the streets of Indore. You're always on the gas; just steer, drift and throw stuff.

  • 🕹️ Steer: put your left thumb on the joystick (or anywhere on the left half) and push left or right. It follows your thumb, like Mario Kart on a phone.
  • 💨 Drift: hold DRIFT while turning. The sparks go white, yellow, orange, then pink. Let go for a boost: the longer the drift, the bigger the boost.
  • 🚀 Rocket start: tap DRIFT just before the lights say PON PON.
  • 🎁 Mithai boxes: drive through one to get an item, then tap the item button.
